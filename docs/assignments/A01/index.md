Scissors are a very commonly used, everyday household item. Handheld scissors are used for cutting soft material such as paper, string, or fabric. Scissors are made of two blades that rotate about a single pivot point acting as a fulcrum. In this way, scissors employ leverage by creating distance between the handles and the blades. This allows the force input by the user to be multiplied by the distance at which the material is being cut, giving it a higher force than was put in. Different sizes of scissors are made depending on the application and the necessary cutting force needed.

**Blades**
Scissors consist of a set of two mirrored blades. The blades are offset to be able to slide next to each other in order to cut material and rest directly next to over top each other when the scissors are closed. The "inside" portion of the blades or the parts of the blades facing each other, are beveled to a sharp point. These points do the actual cutting. As the scissors close, the material between the blades gets pierced and cut by the sharp point. 

**Handles**
The handles are attached to the opposite end of the blades and are the part the user interacts with. The handles are loops on each blade that the user puts their fingers and thumb into to be able to operate the scissors. Depending on the brand of scissors, one handle loop may be longer to be able to be able to fit the users fingers, providing a better grip. There are also left and right-handed scissors to allow everyone to be able to easily use scissors.

**Pivot**
The blades are attached at a single common point, usually a screw or rivet, about which the blade and handle assemblies can rotate. Depending on the type of scissors, the pivot point may be more or less offset, but it is generally placed around the midpoint between the blades and handles. The pivot is what gives the blades the leverage needed to perform the cut by acting as a fulcrum with the blades as the levers. The pivot point is allows the scissors to be opened and closed to be able to actually perform a cut. A user simply rotates the handles which opens the blades between which the desired material to be cut can be placed. The handles are then rotated back, closing the blades around the material, performing the cut.

Knives and shears are common alternatives to scissors. Both have advantages and disadvantages when compared to scissors. Knives are able to cut a wider variety of materials and are not limited to a specific method of cutting. However, knives can be less precise and sometimes require a surface to cut on. Shears are similar to scissors, utilizing blades that pivot around a common point. Although, shears are generally much larger and more purpose built for applications that require more cutting force, such as garden work.

A decision made by Westcott Rule Co. was to coat the scissors with a titanium based coating. The purpose of the titanium coating is to improve resistance to wear, rust, and damage. The coating allows the scissors to last longer, maintaining good performance and quality of cuts, and improves the visual appearance. The coating has been very successful and Westcott now uses it on all of the blades and cutting tool the company produces.
